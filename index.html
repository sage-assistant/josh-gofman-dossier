Section 2 // Operational History

The Gofman Timeline

How a Canadian yoga instructor became one of the most influential figures in Cape Town's culinary underground.

Phase 1 — Ontario, Canada
The Yoga Cover
Subject establishes WYNN Yoga in Thornhill, Ontario. Grows it into the largest yoga studio in the city, the only one with a fitness offering. Intelligence analysts now believe this was a front to develop the core flexibility needed to photograph food from impossible angles. The "WYNN" acronym ("What You Need Now") is later identified as a subliminal food prompt.
Phase 2 — The Pivot
Activation Event
Subject abruptly transitions from yoga to restaurant technology. When questioned, claims he "wanted to help restaurants." Intelligence community remains skeptical. Nobody leaves yoga for menus unless they're running from something or toward something much bigger.
Phase 3 — EatApp Founded
Operation Digital Menu
Subject creates EatApp Menu, a platform that replaces boring printed menus with interactive digital experiences integrated with POS systems. Within months, restaurants using EatApp report a suspicious 40% increase in dessert orders. The connection is classified.
Phase 4 — Relocation to Cape Town
The Southern Hemisphere Expansion
Subject relocates to Cape Town, South Africa. Motive unclear. Working theories include: the food scene, the weather, the wine, or an elaborate plan to control appetite levels across an entire hemisphere. All theories are considered equally plausible.
Phase 5 — Present Day
Currently At Large
Subject continues to operate freely in Cape Town, making restaurants look better than they've ever looked and making everyone around him unreasonably hungry. Multiple attempts to contain his influence have failed. He keeps launching features.
